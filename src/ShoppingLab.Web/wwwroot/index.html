Limitations

  • All products, brands and GTINs are fictional (GTIN prefix 200 is reserved for in-store use).
  • The feed uses the field names of OpenAI's products file-upload spec; standard OpenAI uploads currently target the US and direct feeds require partner onboarding. Prices here are in PLN for a Polish demo store.
  • The local search is a rule-based baseline. LLM results depend on model, prompt and time and are observations, not controlled evidence.
  • robots.txt and sitemap.xml are served per scenario under a sub-path to keep both shops side by side; on a real site they live at the domain root.
